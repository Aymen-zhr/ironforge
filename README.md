## ⚡ Introduction

**IronForge** is a personal strength and human performance workstation designed for athletes and lifters who demand precision without distraction. 

Built on the principles of biomechanics, progressive overload, and autonomic recovery, IronForge strips away the noise and spreadsheet bloat of conventional fitness trackers to deliver a focused, tactile training experience.

## 🏛️ The Four Pillars

### 1. The Forge
Pure set-by-set execution. Record loads, reps, and rate of perceived exertion (RPE) with instant previous-performance benchmarks and real-time barbell plate calculations.

### 2. The Fuel
Caloric and macronutrient tracking centered around real performance. Monitor daily energy balance, protein thresholds, and nutritional momentum through intuitive visual telemetry.

### 3. The Restore
Recovery is where adaptation happens. Track systemic readiness, sleep quality, localized muscle recovery, and hydration balance to ensure your body is primed for the next training session.

### 4. The Trajectory
Visualizing progress over weeks and months. Follow a dynamic progression curve that projects weight and composition goals alongside your personal record vault.
